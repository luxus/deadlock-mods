# Changelog

All notable changes to Deadlock Zipline Boost are documented here. A release takes its
notes from its section here.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-25

### Changed

- The vendored Addon Settings library moves from 1.2.4 (`BUILD` 10) to 1.2.6 (`BUILD` 13): the ADDONS
  list grows to 880 px before it scrolls, multi-layout registrations work (1.2.5), and the Activated
  bubble recursion and the alias-unset-on-takeover are fixed (1.2.6).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-23

- The mod as it moved into this repository: an always-visible Ready / Active / Cooldown timer for the
  zipline speed boost, a settings tab in CompLock HUD++ or the ADDONS window, and localization.
- Earlier history is in the [deadlock-qol-mods repository](https://github.com/luxus/deadlock-qol-mods).
